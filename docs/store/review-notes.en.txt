The interface is available in 14 languages; course lessons are original
English and Turkish text. Browse without signing in. Gravita teaches five
skill areas in 7 written courses with 117 lessons: the 56-lesson calisthenics
course, three dumbbell / kettlebell paths (written route, handling,
transitions) and three 12-lesson foundation courses for barbell, low rings
and hand support. The 24 physical tasks of the three foundation courses remain
held pending expert review, permitted demonstrations and observed learner
acceptance. Reading a lesson does not unlock a held task. Inversions are not
taught.

Subscriptions. Gravita offers an optional auto-renewing subscription
("Gravita", monthly or yearly) and the shared "Motion+" subscription that also
unlocks the same tier in the other Motion apps by the same developer.
Free without an account: the first part of every course, 44 of 117 lessons
(calisthenics 21 of 56, dumbbell / kettlebell written route 6 of 12, handling
4 of 11, transitions 1 of 2, barbell, rings and hand support 4 of 12 each),
plus progress, saved program and history, which always stay readable.
Subscription unlocks: the remaining 73 lessons and independent practice
(creating the eight-week program and logging new sessions). A locked lesson
shows a lock icon; opening it asks the reviewer to sign in or opens the
membership options. Prices come from
the store; a free trial is shown only when the store offers one to this
account. Purchases require a Motion account so access can be restored on
another device; "Restore purchases" and "Manage subscription" (opens the store
subscription settings) are on Account > Membership. Accounts that declare an
age under 13 cannot be created; accounts for 13–17 that still await parental
consent are not offered purchases. Camera coaching, live measurement and form
grades are disabled in this version and are not part of the subscription.
Sandbox test account: provided in App Store Connect review fields.

Open a course, read its prerequisites, equipment, regression, progression and
rest guidance. Reading progress is stored on this device. Guest progress is
separate from each signed-in account and is not migrated when signing in. This
progress is a practice record, not a mastery or form score. Demonstration
videos are not supplied in this version. The lessons do not need camera or
microphone permission.

The optional shared Motion account supports sign-in/profile, membership and
account deletion. For account-specific review, approved test credentials must
be provided in the stores' secure review fields after real-device validation.
Deleting an account affects the shared Motion account across all five apps;
it does not cancel any store subscription.
Do not delete a real user's account for a review demonstration. A separately
approved disposable account is needed for the destructive end-to-end deletion
test.
